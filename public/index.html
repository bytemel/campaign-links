Why not one link for everything?

One link tells you how many people clicked, not where they were. A link per channel answers the question a programme team argues about every intake: was it the WhatsApp blast, the LinkedIn post or the posters?

Click counters also lie. Paste a link in a WhatsApp group and WhatsApp's servers fetch it to draw the preview. LinkedIn, Slack and X do the same. Those fetches are counted here as previews, never as people.

Each link adds utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign, so Google Analytics or your form tool sees the channel too.
